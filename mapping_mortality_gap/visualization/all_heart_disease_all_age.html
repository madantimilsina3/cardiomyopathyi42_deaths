Reading this chart correctly

Relative weight, not total volume. Cardiomyopathy still kills more people aged 65+ than any other age group in raw numbers — 93,236 deaths from 2018–2024, versus 1,713 among 0–24‑year‑olds. What this chart shows is the share of each age group's heart-disease deaths that Cardiomyopathy accounts for: about 58% in the 0–24 group versus roughly 3.5% in the 65+ group. Because heart disease is so much rarer in young people overall, Cardiomyopathy makes up a much larger slice of a much smaller pie — which is exactly why it deserves closer attention as a threat to younger adults, even though it is a minor contributor to the (far larger) total burden of heart disease in older adults.
