Seven years of cardiomyopathy mortality, by sex and by season

Adult deaths attributed to I42 (cardiomyopathy), 2018–2024. The first view tracks the annual mortality rate by sex; the second lays each year's monthly death count on top of the next to surface seasonal rhythm. Hover any line for exact figures, click a legend chip to isolate a series.

01 — By sex

Male mortality runs roughly double the female rate — and both are declining

The male mortality rate fell from 6.4 to 5.3 per 100k (−17.2%) between 2018 and 2024. The female rate fell more modestly, from 3.0 to 2.6 per 100k (−13.3%), holding the gap between the two lines roughly constant across the period.

02 — By season, year over year

Deaths cluster in deep winter, with a secondary lift each July
